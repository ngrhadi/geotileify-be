Privacy Policy

Geotileify converts geospatial files into streaming PMTiles. This page explains what data is processed when you use the website, API, and browser extension.

LAST UPDATED · 28 OCTOBER 2026

This policy applies to the Geotileify landing page, the /generate API, and the Chrome extension. If you use a self-hosted Geotileify deployment, the operator of that deployment is responsible for its data practices.

1. Data we process

Uploaded files

When you submit a geospatial file, we receive its content, filename, size, and the format and tiling options you select. Uploaded files may contain personal data, for example property names, addresses, or coordinates. Only upload data you have the right to process.

Generated output

We generate a .pmtiles archive, a public ULID, and a short-lived signed download or streaming URL.

Technical data

Like most web services, we may process your IP address, request time, endpoint, user agent, status code, and error information for security, rate limiting, and operations.

Browser extension

The extension sends the file you choose to the Geotileify backend configured in the build. It does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The optional map preview loads base map tiles from Thunderforest.

2. How we use data

  • Convert uploaded files and serve generated PMTiles or downloads.
  • Enforce upload limits and IP-based rate limits, and prevent abuse.
  • Debug failures and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build user profiles. The service does not require an account.

3. Storage and retention

  • Tiles: uploaded files and generated archives are stored in S3-compatible object storage (MinIO) and expire after 24 hours.
  • Cleanup: a background job runs every 15 minutes to delete expired objects and their PostgreSQL metadata rows.
  • Signed URLs: download and streaming links are short-lived and expire after 5 minutes.
  • Temporary files: conversion workspaces are removed when the request finishes.
  • Logs: operational logs may be retained according to the hosting provider's configuration.

Because the service is ephemeral, a file may no longer be recoverable after its 24-hour window and cleanup pass.

4. Third-party services

Depending on how you use Geotileify, requests may be sent to the following providers, which receive the technical data needed to serve the request under their own privacy policies:

  • Object storage (MinIO): stores uploaded and generated files.
  • Thunderforest: serves base map tiles in the optional map preview.
  • Google Fonts: serves the Geist and Geist Mono web fonts on the website.
  • Chrome Web Store: distributes the browser extension.
  • GitHub: hosts the source code and handles issues and support requests.

5. Cookies and tracking

The landing page and API do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The extension does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Files downloaded through the extension are handled by Chrome's download manager.

6. Security

Geotileify uses signed URLs, access controls, upload size limits, rate limiting, and zip safety checks. Production deployments should serve traffic over HTTPS. No system is completely secure, so please do not upload sensitive personal data.

7. Deletion and your choices

Uploads expire automatically after 24 hours. To ask a privacy question or request deletion before expiry, open an issue at github.com/ngrhadi/geotileify-be/issues. Include the tile ID if you have it; we may need to verify the request. If you use a self-hosted deployment, contact that deployment's operator.

8. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). Do not upload children's personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected on this page or in the project repository.

10. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or privacy concerns:

  • Issues: github.com/ngrhadi/geotileify-be/issues
  • Source: github.com/ngrhadi/geotileify-be
